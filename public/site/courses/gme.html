About this pathway

Complete B.Tech / B.E. in Mechanical Engineering (Any Mechanical Engineering), then join Graduate Marine Engineering (GME) — 1 year. Complete mandatory sea training, appear for MEO Class IV exam (as per DGS requirements), then start as 3rd Engineer and grow to Chief Engineer.
