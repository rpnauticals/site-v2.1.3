GP Rating — Ordinary Seaman (OS)

Work on global ships. Gain real experience. Build a rewarding career at sea through deck operations and seamanship.

Ordinary Seaman basic requirements
Education10th Pass (Minimum)
Age18 – 25 years (age relaxation for SC/ST as per DGS rules)
MedicalMedically fit with required eyesight and colour vision (as per DGS standards)
DocumentsValid passport, CDC (after course), other required documents
NationalityIndian (or as per company requirement)

Course highlights

  • DG Shipping Approved GP Rating Course
  • 6 Months Residential Training
  • Deck Operations & Seamanship Training
  • Practical learning and hands-on training
  • STCW Basic Safety Training (Included)
  • Documentation & Interview Guidance
  • Placement Assistance with Reputed Companies
