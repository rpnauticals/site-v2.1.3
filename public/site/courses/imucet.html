What is IMU-CET?

The Indian Maritime University Common Entrance Test is India's national maritime university entrance exam, for admission to maritime programmes through the IMU network, such as B.Tech Marine Engineering and B.Sc Nautical Science.

Who can apply?

Class 12 pass with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics, as per programme norms. Appearing students, 12th-passed candidates and JEE aspirants can prepare with us. The exam language is English.

Confirm eligibility and exam details in the official IMU notification for your chosen programme.
