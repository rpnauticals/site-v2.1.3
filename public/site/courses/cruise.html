Cruise Ship Photographer

Learn, practice and get placement support to start your career as a professional photographer with top international cruise lines.

Cruise Ship Photography basic requirements
AgeMinimum 18 to 21 years (depending on the cruise line)
Experience6 months to 1 year of general or event photography experience (weddings, portraits, or social events)
LanguageFluency in English is mandatory. Additional European languages (German, Spanish, Italian or French) are a major plus.
SkillsComfort with DSLR cameras, external flashes and basic editing tools like Adobe Photoshop or Lightroom.

Course highlights

  • Complete photography training (from basics to advanced)
  • Editing classes (Photoshop & Lightroom)
  • Latest cameras, lenses & accessories provided for training
  • Live practice sessions, portfolio building & mock assignments
  • Personality development & guest interaction training
  • Interview preparation & placement support
  • Learn from industry experts with real cruise experience

Cruise Ship Gym Trainer

Stay fit. Inspire others. See the world — turn your passion for fitness into a global cruise ship career.

Cruise Ship Gym Trainer basic requirements
Education12th Pass (Minimum)
Age21 – 35 years (age relaxation may be available depending on the company)
CertificationRecognised Personal Trainer / Fitness Certification (such as ACE, NASM, ISSA, REPs or equivalent)
MedicalMedically fit as per company requirements (ENG1 medical)
DocumentsValid passport, CDC (if required by the company) and other related documents

Our support & facilities

  • English Classes — Spoken and Maritime English
  • Personality Development — confidence and professional communication
  • Interview Preparation — mock interviews and cruise company guidance
  • Marine Test Preparation — aptitude and company tests
  • Documentation & Visa Guidance — full joining-process support
  • Placement Assistance — help connecting with reputed cruise lines
