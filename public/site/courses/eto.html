Your journey to become an ETO

4 clear steps

  1. 1
    Step 1

    College Selection & Entrance

    Get admitted

    Apply to a DG Shipping-approved college (like HIMT, Tolani (TMI), AMET, etc.), clear the online entrance exam and interview, and pass the DG-approved medical fitness test.

  2. 2
    Step 2

    4-Month Campus Training

    Residential programme

    Strictly residential / hostel-based programme. Learn marine electrical systems, switchboards, automation, control systems and safety drills with hands-on practical training.

  3. 3
    Step 3

    Document Collection

    After the course

    After successful completion of the course, the college will issue your INDoS Number; obtain your CDC (Seaman's Passport).

  4. 4
    Step 4

    Onboard Training (Sea Service)

    Join as Trainee ETO

    Apply to shipping companies for placement, join as a Trainee ETO and complete 8 months of onboard training on a commercial ship.
