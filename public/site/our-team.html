Director
Kaushal Dhillon

"I didn't want RP Nauticals to be another coaching center. I wanted it to be the guide I wish I'd had before I first stepped onto a ship."

With hands-on experience across leading names in global shipping — including Hapag-Lloyd, A.P. Moller-Maersk, and Campbell Shipping Group — Kaushal has seen the industry from the inside and now channels that experience into helping the next generation of seafarers get it right from day one.

Business Associate

Operations Executive
Esha Kumari

By eliminating process inefficiencies and removing barriers to entry, I implement clear, standardized frameworks that guide students from initial qualification through to successful professional placement.
